08 Questions

        

          Questions we hear
          before every build.
        

      

      

        
How much does a project cost?
Every project gets a fixed quote after a free discovery call. You pay for agreed scope and milestones, never for open ended hours.

        
How long does a typical build take?
A marketing site usually takes two to four weeks. A web or mobile app with a backend usually takes eight to sixteen. You get a dated plan before any work starts.

        
Who owns the code?
You do. The repository lives in your GitHub organisation from day one, and full ownership transfers on final payment.

        
Can you take over an existing codebase?
Yes. We start with a short audit, fix what is risky first, then keep shipping features on top of what you already have.

        
Do you design as well as build?
Yes. Product design, UI, motion and 3D are done by the same team that writes the code, so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

        
Which technologies do you use?
Whatever fits the product. Usually React or Next.js on the web, Flutter or React Native on mobile, and Node, Python or Go with Postgres on the backend.

        
What happens after launch?
Monitoring, bug fixes and performance checks continue for 30 days at no cost. After that you can move to a monthly care plan or take it in house.

        
Do you work across time zones?
Yes. We overlap at least four hours with your working day and share progress in writing the rest of the time, so nothing waits on a meeting.
